One review loop

Callysto begins with the smallest sequence that can improve a computational claim. It does not add execution, votes, forks, or ranking to the pilot.

1 · Publish

An invited owner publishes one immutable notebook version and a question for reviewers.

→
2 · Inspect and discuss

A reviewer reads saved cells and outputs, then comments on the exact version and cell.

→
3 · Respond or revise

The owner responds, marks the issue addressed, or publishes a linked new version.

Trust is stated narrowly

Rendering saved notebook content is not the same as running, reproducing, peer reviewing, or verifying an analysis.

Product demonstration of Callysto trust language
Callysto may show Format valid, render complete, immutable version identity, and the public review record.
Callysto does not claim That the notebook ran successfully, reproduced a result, passed peer review, or is scientifically correct.
Reader boundary Notebook content is author-supplied. Rich outputs are isolated and unsupported output remains visibly unsupported.
