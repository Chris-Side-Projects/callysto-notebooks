Owner decisions this set is meant to resolve

  1. Does the light editorial system feel serious and specific enough for computational review?
  2. Does the homepage explain the product and review loop within one first-screen composition?
  3. Does the desktop review rail preserve notebook focus while keeping discussion discoverable?
  4. Does inline mobile discussion maintain enough cell context without making the document feel fragmented?
  5. Are the processing and failure states calm, honest, and sufficiently actionable?
  6. Is the Product demonstration treatment impossible to mistake for production evidence?

Static controls intentionally do not mutate data. See README.md for review boundaries, accessibility notes, and the approval checklist.
